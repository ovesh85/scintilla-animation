The honest part

What people ask before they buy.

What if it looks different in person?

That is the fear that stops most people, and it is fair. Photographs flatten a stone. So we put the piece on this page moving, turning, and apart, and we still expect you to see it in your hand before you commit. Your viewing is free and you can walk away from it.

Can I really swim in it?

Yes. Salt water does nothing to platinum or to 18 carat gold, and a well set stone does not move. Rinse it in fresh water afterwards, the same as you would your own skin. Sun cream is the only thing that dulls it, and that washes off.

How do I know it is worth the price?

Every piece ships with its grading report from an independent lab, the cutter's name, and the four weeks of work logged by date. You can take all of it to any appraiser you like. We would rather you did.

Can it be resized?

Yes, once, free, at any point in the first year. After that it is charged at cost, which is usually under two hundred dollars.

What if I change my mind?

Thirty days, worn or not, full refund. You pay nothing to return it. We have had four come back in six years.

Who actually makes these?

One cutter and two setters in a workshop of five people. You will meet whoever made yours at the viewing, and they will tell you what fought them.
